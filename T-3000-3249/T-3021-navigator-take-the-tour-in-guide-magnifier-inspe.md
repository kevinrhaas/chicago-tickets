*"there should be an option to take the tour anytime even if you skip it, that menu that comes up only comes up onece and then you cant take the tour again as far as i can find so take the tour should be either at the main card, or in help or in settings, whatever is the most logical in your view, and there are two icons that look like houses etierh change the home icon to something else or make the inspect something like a magnifying glass ... and on this tab i would think that the move walking and confidence view would go to those settings"*;
*"and you cant navigate back from here in the building view"* (Inspect › Design scrolled, the tab row gone);
*"some cards on the left have people design story tabs and this one is different? There should be some consistency in the cards"* (Glessner scrolled: its tab row had scrolled off the top).

**Acceptance:**
- Guide (the ? app) opens with a plain **Take the tour** key at its top that starts the Field Check at step 01, whether the tour was skipped, finished or never offered.
- Inspect's rail icon (and the HUD tag's Inspect key) is a magnifying glass, not a house; Home keeps the house.
- Home's MOVE and CONFIDENCE VIEW tiles are buttons that open the Move app and the Confidence view app.
- Inspect's tab row stays pinned at the top of the screen while its pane scrolls, and wraps so all six tabs show at 400 px; Inspect carries a Back key to the app it was opened from (Home when none).
- Desktop 1280×800 and phone 390×780, four appearances.
