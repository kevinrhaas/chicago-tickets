Navigator: fold the building card's research into Inspect behind an Expert research setting.

The owner, 2026-10-22 (same thread): *"i live these inspect cards but i am wondering what's next in either integrating those detailed researcher/educator cards into the navidgator, so maybe there are additional tabs that are hidden or another menu item with those in the navigator app, all of that information should go there and be a setting for the user on can they see the expert research or not in the navigator, because then it would simplify ... i like the existing cards so dont want to complicate that but the detailed research card needs to be folded into that"*.

**Acceptance:**
- The existing Researcher mode switch is the setting: named Expert research, placed at the top of Settings, off by default, remembered per device.
- With it on, Inspect shows a Research tab carrying what the right-hand card holds beyond Inspect's own tabs: the evidence (basis, where it stood, was it here, shape, attributes with their why, citations), liberties and open questions, and the record (dossier link, phase). With it off the tab is absent and Inspect is unchanged.
- A click or tap on a building in the scene opens it in Inspect, not the right-hand card; Full record opens the Research tab (or says where to turn Expert research on). Non-building picks (street grid, trees, context city, railway) keep the small card.
- The tour's Select / Release steps still complete on the new path.
- Desktop and phone, four appearances.
