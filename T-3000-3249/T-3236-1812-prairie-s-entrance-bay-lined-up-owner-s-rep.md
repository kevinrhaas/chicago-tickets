## Owner's report (2026-10-28, project thread "Align 1812 Prairie north entrance bay", with a screenshot looking up at the entrance from the stair)

> and here on 1812 prairie, the windows are not aligned/centered correctly on the north side of the building. the top window is close but the 2nd story is off, and the door is not centered, and the left edging around the door is not on the face of the north side, like the right edging, and then the south side of the building would not make that jut back, it would be a normal face, so potentially some resizing of the south side or the north side to align things neatly, you see what I am saying?, and the door is a bit hard to see, it looks flat, and the decoration above the door the window needs to be aligned and adjustted and matched and centered correctly

**Acceptance:** from the street and from the stair, the north bay's second-storey window, the small arched window over it and the porch arch stand on one axis at the bay's middle (between the projection's north face and the north corner); the arch's moulding stands on the bay's own face with equal stone piers each side and the projection's corner is whole (no notch); the door reads with depth (set back, panelled, dark); the fanlight's bars are even under the arch. Both match records carry the departure from the photographs (which set the bay about 0.3 m south and cut the arch into the corner) as an owner ruling, with a liberty.
