The owner, 2026-10-22: "you have the top image for the mcbirney house at 1736, you have it mixed up with the other mcbirney house".

The 1736 card's top picture is the January 1890 Inland Architect plate "Residence for Hugh J. McBirney" (Burling & Whitehouse), which is the son's new Tudor stone house at 1625 Prairie. 1736 is the elder Hugh McBirney's 1864 Ingraham house (Second Empire, veranda front, c. 1890 photograph). The library record `a16-inland-1890-hj-mcbirney-residence` keeps the Art Institute's wrong catalogue address "1736 S. Prairie Avenue (… probably wrong)" in its `addresses`, and build_images.py places a record on every lot its addresses name, so the plate lands on `prairie_1736`, on the card, and in 1736's structure grade ("Pictured: 2"). The June 1889 American Architect permit for H. J. McBirney's three-storey Burling & Whitehouse dwelling (`dw-aabn-1889-mcbirney-1736-permit`) is the same 1625 house and is also filed under 1736.

**Acceptance:**
- the plate (and its Chicagology copy) shows on 1625's card only, not on 1736's; 1736's top picture is its own c. 1890 photograph
- the 1889 permit is filed under 1625, not 1736
- 1736's grade lists only pictures of 1736
- the 1736 model is checked against the picture it was built from (it was built from the c. 1890 photograph, `glessner_blog_1736_mcbirney_c1890_photo`, not the plate)
