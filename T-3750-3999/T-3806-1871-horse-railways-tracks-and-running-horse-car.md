**Where:** the streets round every 1871 jaunt stop (The Great Chicago Fire, The Last Sunday, A Message for the Opera House, Who Started the Fire?): the detail rings in `data/context/1871/detail-rings.json` (Court House square, State Street Lake to Monroe, Dearborn and Madison, Washington State to Dearborn, Lake and Clark, DeKoven at Jefferson, Chicago Avenue at Pine, the Clark and State bridges), extended where a stop looks down a street the rings miss. Photographic only round the stops, cheap elsewhere; Bill Tyre's UHD 630 and the phone stay smooth (light tier inside its ceiling); site storage budget respected.

**References:** `/mnt/project-files/1871-references/` (Lake Street stereoview late 1860s, Kurz LaSalle from Court House square 1865, from the Court House cupola c1858, State Street horse cars, Library Hall), the LOC stereographs already in `data/sources/` (1s12325 Court House, 1s12371 Sherman House, 1s47902 Crosby's, 1s47905 Tremont), Andreas vol. 2 plates, and `edwards_chicago_directory_1871` (full text: archive.org edwardsannualdir141871chic, djvu.txt).

**What:** Chicago's horse railways in October 1871: the Chicago City Railway (State Street, and its other South Side lines), the Chicago West Division Railway (Madison, Randolph, Lake and the other West Side lines) and the North Chicago City Railway (Clark, Wells and the North Side). Edwards' 1871 directory and the period maps give the routes.

- Tracks in the roadway (gauge, strap or flat rail flush with the paving, the turnouts and the downtown loops/terminals), drawn on the 1871 streets.
- Horse cars running on them on a timetable: period car bodies (closed cars with platforms and clerestory, the lines' own colours and lettering where a source gives them), one or two horses, driver and conductor from the people pipeline; open cars only if the season and a source allow.
- The fire jaunt's night: cars stopped or abandoned as the accounts say; stops that name the cars (Last Sunday) show them.

**Acceptance:** tracks on every 1871 horse-car street with a cited route; cars running on the lines near the stops, cheap and LOD-tiered; before/after, desktop and phone; budgets on light.
