**Where:** the streets round every 1871 jaunt stop (The Great Chicago Fire, The Last Sunday, A Message for the Opera House, Who Started the Fire?): the detail rings in `data/context/1871/detail-rings.json` (Court House square, State Street Lake to Monroe, Dearborn and Madison, Washington State to Dearborn, Lake and Clark, DeKoven at Jefferson, Chicago Avenue at Pine, the Clark and State bridges), extended where a stop looks down a street the rings miss. Photographic only round the stops, cheap elsewhere; Bill Tyre's UHD 630 and the phone stay smooth (light tier inside its ceiling); site storage budget respected.

**References:** `/mnt/project-files/1871-references/` (Lake Street stereoview late 1860s, Kurz LaSalle from Court House square 1865, from the Court House cupola c1858, State Street horse cars, Library Hall), the LOC stereographs already in `data/sources/` (1s12325 Court House, 1s12371 Sherman House, 1s47902 Crosby's, 1s47905 Tremont), Andreas vol. 2 plates, and `edwards_chicago_directory_1871` (full text: archive.org edwardsannualdir141871chic, djvu.txt).

**What:** the signs over the stores near the stops are today the same few trade words from an atlas ("never a firm's name"). Make them the real firms of 1871 at their real addresses, as specific and varied as the period photos show.

- Read Edwards' 1871 directory (its business directory by trade, the named blocks list, the street entries) for every firm on the faces round the stops: Lake (Wabash to Wells), Clark (river to Madison and round Court House square), Randolph, Washington (State to LaSalle), State (Lake to Monroe), Dearborn and Madison.
- Place them by the 1871 (pre-1909) numbering, calibrated from the directory's own corner entries ("93 and 94 Lake, cor. Dearborn", "Lake, sw. cor. LaSalle"); record the numbering model as data (reusable by T-3738).
- Sign forms as the stereoviews show them, varied per front: name boards along the cornice or over the storefront, lettering on the lintel, hanging signs at right angles to the walk (the "TELEGRAPH" and "UNION PACIFIC" boards), boards on the piers, gilt window lettering, painted wall and party-wall lettering, awning valances; period faces (Roman, Egyptian/slab, Clarendon, Tuscan, condensed sans), shaded and gilt.
- Grades: a firm at its listed address is attested (directory); its place along the block inferred from the numbering; the sign form, wording beyond the listing and lettering reconstructed (a liberty). Where no listing is found, a trade sign stays a trade sign.

**Acceptance:** every storefront in the detail rings near a stop carries a sign of its own firm or trade, no two neighbours alike; a data file of the firms with directory locators; Inspect shows the firm and its listing; before/after at each stop, desktop and phone; memory and draw budgets measured on light.
