Kevin, 2026-11-26 (project chat, his list, 1904 Prairie Avenue): "Correct alleyway grading and remove
inappropriate curbs at alley entrances."

Seen in the 1904 app on dev: at every one of the district's 13 alley mouths the cross street's
parkway turf and its 7-in curb run straight across the alley, so the alley ends at the lot line on
the lawn's level and a wagon would have to drop a curb into the street.

The 1905 code (sec. 2062) leaves "a space of one and one-half inches ... between all walks and the
curb at street and alley intersections": at an alley the walk stops at a curb, i.e. the curb is
turned into the alley as at a street corner.

**Acceptance:**
- At all 13 alley mouths the parkway and the curb across the alley are gone; the alley's own surface
  runs on across the sidewalk space from the lot line to the curb line.
- That surface falls from the alley's grade at the lot line to the gutter at the curb line, meeting
  the roadway with no step.
- The curb is turned into the alley on both sides (a return curb), its top on the walk's level, its
  face dying out toward the lot line; the walk stops at it.
- Generated by tools/lay_prairie_1904_street_section.py into data/street_section/1904.json, drawn by
  street-grid.js; offsetAt (and so every layer standing on it) reads the new grade; the field's ground
  is cut under it.
- Desktop 1280x800 and phone 390x780 before/after pictures at the mouths, zero page errors; liberty
  recorded.
