Where and when? picture hunts in 1812 and 1871 (1812's river views from a boat).

**Acceptance:** (state it before working — the definition of done, never weakened to pass)

## Result (PR 2362)

1812 has a four-picture hunt of the Kinzie house. Lossing 1869 and Sarony/Currey 1912 were drawn from the river, so those two rounds are guessed from a boat. 1871 has a three-photo hunt: the Chamber of Commerce, the Water Works and the Ogden house. The Court House, Tremont House and O'Leary cameras are left out because each stands inside a footprint that the pre-fire town builds.
