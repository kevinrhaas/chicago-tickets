**What:** the particular scenes the accounts set at the stops' places and hours (each needs its stop's text to match, T-3751's rule):

- The Court House (stop 4, about 1:30-2 a.m.): the prisoners let out of the basement jail, "all bareheaded, many barefooted, rushed into the street", and the truck of ready-made clothing at Randolph Street they emptied (Andreas p. 725).
- The North Side sands (Water Works / Ogden house stops): people "standing in the water at various depths, from their knees to their waists, all with their backs to the storm of fire", horses driven into the lake, the grounds "dotted all over ... with piles of trunks, chairs, tables, beds" (Andreas p. 744).
- Men on roofs with buckets wetting them against the sparks (Andreas pp. 742-743).
- The gunpowder at Harrison Street and Wabash on Monday (Andreas pp. 735-739), if a stop is added there or the State Street stop's hour allows it.

**Acceptance:** each scene staged where its stop's words or hour put it, student-appropriate in student tellings; before/after pictures desktop and phone.
