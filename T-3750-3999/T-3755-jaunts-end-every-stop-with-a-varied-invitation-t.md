Jaunts end every stop with a varied invitation; the Fort Dearborn ending keeps time order.

The owner, 2026-11-26 (project chat, "Jaunts – General Improvements" and "1812 – Fort Dearborn"):

- "Narration endings: End each scene's narration with an invitation to proceed to the next stop or explore the current location. Vary the language so it does not become repetitive. This may involve some re-recording do what you can to minimize this so we save eleven labs tokens"
- "Fort Dearborn jaunt: Correct the awkward chronological transition at the end. The narration jumps backward in time, saying the fort was burned the following day after the user has already witnessed it burning."

**Acceptance:**
- Every stop of every jaunt, every year and every telling, ends with a closing line that invites the visitor on to the next stop or to look around here (a stop asking for a choice: to choose; the last stop: a last look). Lines come from one shared, varied set, chosen so no line repeats within a jaunt until the set runs out, simpler words for ages 8-10, and Spanish where the jaunt is read in Spanish.
- The line is shown on the stop's card and, in a voiced telling, read by the narrator straight after the stop's own voice. No stop is re-recorded: each line is voiced once and shared by every jaunt that uses it (the voicer never voices one line twice in a run).
- The Battle of Fort Dearborn ending (every telling, 3-5 to 18+, and Spanish) follows the burning the visitor has just watched instead of starting again at "the next morning".
- Gate green; desktop 1280×800 and phone 390×780 pictures.
