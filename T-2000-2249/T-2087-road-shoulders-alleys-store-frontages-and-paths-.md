Road shoulders, alleys, store frontages and paths go to trodden earth grading into turf, replacing the prairie left in the 80-foot corridor beside the wagon track.

## FILED OVER THE BUDGET, AND HERE IS THE REASON

The ticket budget refused this: the queue stands at 190 lines, at or over its ceiling of 140. It was filed anyway, on this reason:

> Owner asked on 2026-10-04 for detailed tickets for tidier town ground cover, placed at the top of the queue; he prefers several well-scoped tickets filed now over one split later

## Why this ticket exists (owner, 2026-10-04)

Fourth of four for the owner's town-ground ask. Kevin named "the sides of the roads", "the alleys, any frontages or paths" as places that still read as dense prairie. T-2084, T-2085 and T-2086 settle the town's ground, draw short turf cheaply and keep the lots; this one finishes the strips between the lots and the wagon track.

## What the code does today

- `streets.js` `blocksGrowth()` (~2027) clears plants only off the worn track plus a 0.65 m shoulder, and its own comment says it keeps "the grassy remainder of the 80-foot corridor". On a 24.4 m corridor with a 10.5 m track (South Water, Lake) that is about 6 m of prairie on each side of the road, between the wagon ruts and the plank walk or lot line.
- Alleys: `tools/generate_plat_lots.py` builds the blocks on the 18-ft alley module (`ALLEY_FT`, ~74) and its header says nothing in `data/sources/` records which blocks were alleyed or where the alley ran; `data/streets/1835.json` carries only two alley records (the Michigan Street tract). Wherever the plat leaves an alley strip and nothing records it, it currently grows prairie.
- Frontages: `data/frontage/town_street_edge.json` (generated by `tools/generate_frontage_works.py`, drawn by `frontage.js`) puts plank walks, crossings, board fences and hitching posts at the lot line on South Water, Lake, Randolph and Washington. Door paths already use `yards.js`'s `road_earth` treatment (T-1984, T-2013), which samples the road's own grit so a path meets the street with no seam.

## The work

1. **The corridor shoulder**: between the track and the lot line, a worn verge that grades from the road's dirt (the `road_earth` grit, no new texture) through trodden ground into T-2085's short turf at the lot line. Hitching posts and plank walks stand in trodden earth; a few weeds survive at post bases and along the walk's edge. Off-street the corridor narrows to a two-rut track in turf, which is what the plat's unopened or lightly used streets were.
2. **Alleys**: where the plat model already has an alley strip, draw it as a trodden lane (worn earth in the middle, turf at the sides). Do not invent alleys the plat does not place: the generator's own caveat stands, and if an alley is drawn on the module alone, that is `reconstructed` and recorded as a liberty (next free L number taken at merge time, not now).
3. **Store frontages**: the ground between a trading frontage's walk and its door, and in front of stores with no walk, is trodden earth with no prairie, using the same `road_earth` path.
4. **Paths**: the door paths and the paths across lots to wells and outbuildings (T-2086) meet the shoulder with no seam.
5. Like T-2084..T-2086, every one of these is a record or a derivation re-run by `check.sh`, never a hand-placed patch, so the next district built gets it automatically.

## Acceptance

- Before/after at both viewports at the road-edge and storefront poses (from T-2084) and one alley pose: no prairie above ankle height on a shoulder, an alley or a frontage; the street reads as a used street with its edges trodden, not a cart track cut through a meadow.
- Triangles and draw calls reported at all tiers against T-2086's after-reading; neither rises; phone heap does not rise. Smoke green at both viewports.
- Changelog entry; liberties recorded where the treatment is reconstructed.
