Teacher routes: pick a jaunt or string stops and people into a route with questions, shared as one link or file.

**Acceptance:** A teacher, in the browser with no server, can (a) take an existing jaunt or (b) string places (buildings) and people of one year into their own route, in order, add a short note and a question to any stop, title it, and get ONE share link (the route encoded in the URL, building on the PR 2197 moment-link rules) and a downloadable route file that can be opened again. A student opening the link lands in that year with a Route panel: stops in order, Go there for each place and person, the teacher's note and question shown as the teacher's words (never as a source claim), and answers saved to the local field notebook for export. Malformed, over-long, wrong-year or unknown-id links degrade to the town with a visible message, never an error. Bounded sizes; no account, upload, tracking or submission service. Works by keyboard and at 390x780 and 1280x800; pictures of both in the PR.
