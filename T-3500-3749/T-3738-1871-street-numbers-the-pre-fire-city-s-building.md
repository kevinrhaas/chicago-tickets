1871 street numbers: the pre-fire city's buildings show their 1871 numbers (old numbering, before the 1909 change) as the period's photographs and directories show them, on the same atlas as 1904's (later; after T-3737).

Kevin, 2026-11-23 (thread "Add period street numbers to buildings"): "yes start with 1904, the other years can come later, you can add tickets for the other years for later to the queue".

**Why only 1871.** Chicago's houses and stores were numbered by 1871 (the old system, renumbered in 1909-11), and the 1871 directories print them. The 1835 town and 1812 had no house numbers: buildings were placed by lot and block or by a landmark, so those years get none, and nothing is invented for them.

**Acceptance:**
- Each named 1871 building whose number a directory or picture of the time gives shows it, in the form a picture of it or of its block shows (a painted figure on a sign board, a transom, a pier or a door); unpictured forms are reconstructed and recorded as a liberty.
- The context city's unnamed fronts get no number unless one is read for that front.
- Reuses T-3737's atlas and layer: one draw, nothing past a short distance, phone boot unchanged.
- Desktop and phone pictures before and after, inline.
