The Last Sunday: a jaunt through 1871 Chicago at its peak on Sunday 8 October, afternoon into dusk, the day before it burned.

**Acceptance:** (state it before working — the definition of done, never weakened to pass)

- 8 to 10 stops on Sunday 8 October 1871, from mid-afternoon to dusk, each with its hour (cited or declared inferred): Lake Street's wholesale row; the Court House square and its cupola; the Sherman House at Clark and Randolph; Potter Palmer's new State Street (Field, Leiter and Co., the Palmer House, open 13 days); Crosby's Opera House, being readied for Theodore Thomas's Monday concert; the Chamber of Commerce; a river crossing with the grain and lumber traffic; the Water Tower and the North Side's fine houses; ending at dusk on DeKoven Street, where the O'Learys' cows are milked, on a hook that leads into the Great Fire jaunt. Default season autumn, dry, warm, windy.
- Told like the 1812 jaunts (every person introduced on first mention, each stop ends on a hook, no technical talk, opening narration on the way to stop 1, closing scene before Outing complete). Staged with the people of the town where the 1871 people (T-3690) are on dev.
- Shares the landmarks of T-3688/T-3689 and the anchors of T-3691; the fire clock stays unset (pre-fire city).
- `menu_group`, `menu_rank`, measured `grade_band`, every band via versions.py (3-5, 6-8, 9-10, 11-12) plus an adults telling; narration direction written (no recording until Kevin asks).
