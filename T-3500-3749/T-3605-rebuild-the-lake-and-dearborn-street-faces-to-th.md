Rebuild the Lake and Dearborn street faces to the Gifford Tremont drawing, then carry its density to the other 1835 streets.

The owner, 2026-11-15 (project thread "Rebuild 1835 streetscapes to Tremont photo"), verbatim:
*"In looking at Tremont house photo and the excellent building match you did there I was noting the rest of the street around it looks different so can to. Look at that image as a reasonable view of 1835 and photo match build the rest of that street face and move replace relocate reconstructed buildings to fill in the accurate streetscape and reconsider the density of that and reapply that level of correct reasonable density and alignment to the rest of the streets in 1835, like south water and all the other major streets likely had a buildings and appearance based on that tremont house image, fill in 1835 and make sure you density the houses and homes and building for complete every resident and building and workplace assigned"*

**Acceptance:**
1. The Gifford drawing (gifford_tremont_house_no_1) is read as a view of 1835 (owner ruling, recorded as a liberty because the drawing is retrospective). Its camera is placed in the town, and the two street faces it shows (Lake Street west of Dearborn, Dearborn Street north of Lake) are rebuilt building by building to it: count, storeys, roof form, frontage width and order, with reconstructed roofs moved or replaced to make room.
2. The same frontage density and alignment is carried to the other principal 1835 business streets (South Water, Lake, and the cross streets near them), by moving or adding reconstructed roofs, with every addition recorded.
3. Every 1835 household has a roof and every workplace a building; the crowding the boarders' deal left is reduced by the added roofs.
4. The 1835 phone memory line (525 MiB) and the site budget hold.

## Done so far

**Phase 1, PR 2093 (merged to dev 2026-11-16).** Nineteen roofs on Lake and Dearborn round the first Tremont House are built to Gifford's drawing, and the Tremont turns its named gable to Lake. Three cottages, the Tremont stable and Bates's auction room moved; none was retired. The roof programme went 668 → 687, the image gate's `gifford_street` view matches, and `waiting_on_a_roof` fell from 25 households to 8.

**Phase 2 (acceptances 2 and 3), PR 2110 (merged to dev 2026-11-16).** The owner chose "Commercial core, about 60 stores". 59 reconstructed stores now stand in the commercial core: 46 on the open block fronts of South Water and Lake, and 13 turning the corner up Wells, LaSalle, Clark and Dearborn (the owner's pick, "Add Clark, Dearborn, Wells, LaSalle"). No house was moved for them: the owner asked for the ten cottages first moved back to be reworked, so a store never stands across a house's dooryard, on a door apron or on a tavern's ell. The roof programme went 687 → 746 and every household has a roof. 1835 phone memory 503.5 of 525 MB.
