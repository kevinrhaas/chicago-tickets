Reading level by age: Settings choice, jaunt filter, level tabs on every card.

Kevin, 2026-11-17: "a person can set a grade level age level in their settings and then the cards they read about people etc are targeted at their age level and they just see their jaunts for their level. i can add multiple levels to see and then you would see all jaunts and also there would be a tab to read the different levels of all of the cards, the default level is the highest level". Ages, not grades, for now (T-3640).

**Acceptance:**
- Settings has a Reading age choice (8–10, 11–13, 14–15, 16–17, 18+), one or several; it is the Jaunts menu's age row too; kept on the device and, signed in, on the account.
- The Jaunts menu shows the tellings for every chosen age (none chosen: each jaunt as first told).
- Every card that holds text written at more than one level shows a tab per level, opening at the highest chosen level; jaunt stops read their other tellings' words.
- Card text at the other levels is written in batches (text only), each measured inside its band.
