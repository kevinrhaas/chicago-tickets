Refresh the first-time tour (Field Check) for today's interface: Interact, Jaunts by grade, labelled rail.

**Acceptance:** the tour matches the interface after T-3629 and names today's main abilities (Interact with people, horses and boats; Jaunts by grade), stays short (one line a step, 14 words or fewer), and works on desktop and phone.

## Done (PR #2158, merged to dev 2026-11-17)

The Field Check is ten steps: Your device, Look around, Walk (Shift / a full thumb push runs), Select a building, Release it, **Interact** (new: walk up to a person, a horse or a boat, press R or tap Interact), **Jaunts** (new: narrated, for your grade), Move, Settings (names the name cards), Ready. People's step gave way to Interact; Ready names People. The card shows a row of step segments and pulses as each step is passed.
