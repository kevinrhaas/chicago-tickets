Who Started the Fire?: a case jaunt at the O'Leary lot that lets the visitor weigh the evidence: the neighbours, the inquiry, the invented cow story and the later theories.

**Acceptance:** (state it before working — the definition of done, never weakened to pass)

- 6 to 8 stops around 137 DeKoven Street and the West Side: the O'Leary cottage and barn on the evening of 8 October (the McLaughlins' party in the front house); Daniel "Peg Leg" Sullivan's account of seeing the barn burn; Dennis Regan; the first alarm and the fire company's wrong turn (watchman Schaffer misjudging the location); the Board of Police and Fire Commissioners' inquiry (November-December 1871), Catherine O'Leary's testimony and the board's finding that the cause could not be determined; reporter Michael Ahern's 1893 admission that the cow story was invented; the later theories (Richard Bales on Sullivan, Anthony DeBartolo on Louis M. Cohn's dice game, the meteor theory as a curiosity); the 1997 City Council resolution clearing Mrs. O'Leary and her cow. Each claim cited; testimony quoted verbatim.
- Ends by asking the visitor to make their case, using the Make your case feature (T-3663/T-3678 machinery) if it is on dev, else a plain closing question.
- Told like the 1812 jaunts, no hedging but fair to each account, "according to legend" for the cow; opening narration and a closing scene. Staged with the O'Learys, Sullivan and the neighbours where the 1871 people (T-3690) are on dev.

- Pictures of every stop inline in the PR, desktop and phone; played end to end in the app. Gate green, merged to dev.
