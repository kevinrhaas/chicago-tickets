1721 Prairie: the 1863 frame Dexter house standing behind Arthur Little's brick front, built to the 1891 plate and the Detroit print.

## Owner's request (Kevin, 2026-11-12, project thread, item 3)

> 3) on 1721 prairie there is a frame house to the rear of the building that should be standing you should be able to see images of it

## What the pictures and maps say

- Wirt Dexter's house of 1863 was frame (Robinson 1886 plate 10 colours it yellow: a long house on the north lot line, set back about 34 ft, stepped on its south side; Inter Ocean 1887 "tastefully constructed frame house"). Arthur Little's brick front of 1889 was built before it, to the street line, and "the frame 1863 house survived behind it".
- The 1891 Inland Architect plate (`a16-inland-1891-dexter-residence`) shows it at the left rear: a clapboarded two-storey gabled block with a bay window on its north side, behind the brick front's north flank.
- The Detroit print of the Pullman house (`a16-pullman-det-4a08065`, 1893-1901) shows it at its left edge: a set-back two-storey gabled house with tall chimneys and a columned porch, between Little's light front and Pullman's.
- The 1911 sheet 20 draws, behind the 3B main block, a "2B" rear range 19.9 x 6.2 m on the north lot line where Robinson's frame house ran, coloured brick, with one-storey frame pieces. The record built it as a flat-decked brick wing "no picture shows".

## Acceptance

- The rear range behind the brick front stands as the 1863 frame house: clapboarded painted timber, two storeys, a slated gable, its chimneys, on the 1911 outline (the nearest map to 1904); the disagreement with the 1911 sheet's colouring recorded in docs/RESEARCH and the liberty.
- The Detroit print's camera solved for `dexter_house_1721_prairie`, rendered with photo_match, and the frame house's differences worked down to `matches` (rule 11 bar); the Inland plate's view re-rendered and still `matches`.
- Visible in the 1904 scene at desktop and 390x780; one What's-New entry.
