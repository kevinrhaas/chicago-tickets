Floating name cards over every person, all years; a tap opens their card.

Kevin, 2026-11-16 (thread "Add floating name cards over people"), verbatim: "Could you add an option setting that is default on that puts a small 3d hovering card floating over each person of what their name is, clicking on the card brings up their detail, make sure the names fit and follow the style of the app" — and, a moment later: "For all years jaunt people and time".

**Acceptance:**
- Settings › Show has a Name cards switch, on by default, stored with the other settings.
- In 1812, 1835 and 1904 every named person drawn in town (on foot, on a driver's box, a jaunt host, a jaunt stop's castmates) carries a small card hovering above their head with their name, at any hour.
- The card sits in the scene (a building in front hides it), faces the visitor, and stays readable as they walk away; it fades out at its reach.
- A click or tap on a card opens that person's own card, as clicking the person does.
- Every name fits on its card (no cut-off), and the card wears the appearance in force (Control Room, Precision Brass, Retro Future, Deep Space), changing when it changes.
- Phones and low-end desktops keep their frame: a capped number of cards within a shorter reach on Light, one draw for all of them, small texture memory.
