People met, places found: a local by-year collection in the People directory and the Map.

**Acceptance:** In each year, this browser keeps a bounded local collection of people actually met and places actually found, by year: met only when the visitor was near the person in town and talked, asked a question or heard a story (opening a card from a directory or search never counts), found only when the walker was physically at or inside the place (never from opening a card or Inspect). The People directory shows who you have met (a badge and a Met filter with a count) and the Map marks places found with a count, both per year. A clear-this-year control, corrupt/unavailable storage handled visibly, no streaks, scores, leaderboards or network. Works at 390x780 and 1280x800; pictures of both in the PR.
