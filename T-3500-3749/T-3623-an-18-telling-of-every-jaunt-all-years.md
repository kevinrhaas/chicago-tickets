An 18+ telling of every jaunt, all years.

Kevin, 2026-11-16: "Do 18+ jaunts for all jaunts, fill them in".

**Acceptance:** every jaunt in 1812, 1835 and 1904, except the build log, has an 18+ telling (`audience: adults`, `version_of` its jaunt). Each keeps its jaunt's stops, sources and title, and puts no grade in the name. Its words read as story, with no warning, no "18+" and no technical note. A non-violent jaunt gets a frank, fuller adult telling, never invented violence. The tellings are text only. Every quotation and fact is backed by the jaunt's own evidence, and the jaunt audits pass. The Jaunts menu can pick the 18+ tellings out once Settings › 18+ jaunts is on.

**Done (PR 2128):** 47 new tellings, built by versions.py from `words/<base>-adults.txt`. With the two 1812 18+ jaunts already there, that makes 50 of 50. The Jaunts menu has an 18+ pill after the grade pills, and each 18+ card sorts last under its jaunt.
