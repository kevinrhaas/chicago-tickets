Kevin, 2026-11-24 (thread "Make the website more cinematic"), following T-3741 (PR 2331).

**Acceptance:** (state it before working — the definition of done, never weakened to pass)
- The reel on the home page reads like a movie trailer: title cards between shots, the complete main title theme (THEME-FINAL, never a clipped jaunt cue) under it, cut to the music.
- A walk filmed at eye level on Prairie Avenue in 1904: from Glessner House south past the Kimball house and one more house south, back north past the Pullman house toward the lake by the conservatory, ending facing back on the Pullman and Kimball corner.
- A new 1812 column film with everyone in it (soldiers, wagons, the children, the families, the Miami escort), the camera sweeping down its side and then over the top.
- A film of people in the street with their name cards and an open person card (the interface visible), used on the site.
- Educators: Google Street View (the Today window, T-3601) shown for class discussion: then and now, side by side, synced to the walk.
- Copy: no "way in" for the audiences; Historians lose "Check our work. All of it." for a plainer headline about research and sources; the "A household, read line by line" person cards are shortened or collapsible.
- Every page has a moving film behind its title (home, audience pages, Contact and Research included), and the home hero is brighter.
- Desktop and 390x780 phone checked with zero page errors; PR body shows the pictures inline.
