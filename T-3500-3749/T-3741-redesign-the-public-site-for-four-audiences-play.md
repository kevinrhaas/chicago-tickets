Redesign the public site for four audiences (players, educators, historians, technicians) with cinematic films, seamless loops and a reliable contact form.

**Acceptance:** (state it before working — the definition of done, never weakened to pass)
- Home: a full-screen film background, the concept in a few lines, four paths (Players, Educators, Historians, Technicians), each year's best shots, and the way in at the bottom. "The town keeps growing", "History you can stand inside" and "Six ways into the town" are gone.
- One page per audience, written for it, with films and pictures; existing feature pages sit under their audience, pages link across rather than repeat. Eras covers 1812, 1835, 1871 and 1904.
- "From a census page to a street you can walk" survives (sources version for historians, a build-pipeline version for technicians).
- Historians drill into 1904: photo matching shown side by side, census research on people, newspaper analyses, grades and liberties.
- Educators: reading levels, every jaunt at every age band, narration, Spanish, understanding checks, teacher routes; server items only as coming.
- Technicians (developers): how it is built and works, how to work with it, the asset and architectural library.
- No looping film jumps at its end (end cross-fades into start); new cinematic fly-mode films with no context-city boxes behind the subject.
- Contact: messages saved in Firebase (owner list page) with a FormSubmit email ping; Contact prominent in the footer.
- Copy: no counts or machinery as selling points, no filler phrases. Desktop and 390x780 phone checked with zero page errors.
