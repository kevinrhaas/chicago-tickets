1812: card the named Native people of the Chicago country who may have been at the camps, each with their sourced story and a likeness.

**Acceptance:** (state it before working — the definition of done, never weakened to pass)
- Named Native people of the Chicago country the sources put in or near Chicago in August 1812 and not yet carded (e.g. Shabbona, Billy Caldwell, Alexander Robinson, Chandonnai, Pierre Leclerc, Co-wa-bee-mai, and the unnamed people of the Kinzie and Helm stories) are carded with their sourced story, presence graded, and a likeness: a portrait where one exists, else a reconstructed look said so.
- Families recorded with them are carded where a source names them; nothing invented is given as evidence.
