"In 1812 there are no bridges so can you in all scenes and areas where we might want to cross like at some of the docks which need to me made photographic wood and at the forks at hardscrabble, at logical places up and down the river provide a small period appropriate boat or canoe that looks photographic correct and allow me to walk up to the boat and interact is board and cross river and you run that sequence, hop in the boat and then you rows across the river and then you hop out of the boat. ## Plan

- 1812 only (its own terrain epoch e1830_natural and river; the 1835 river and boats.js layer are not touched).
- Boats drawn up on the bank at crossings: the fort wharf / Kinzie landing, the forks (Wolf Point), Lee's Place (Hardscrabble) on the South Branch, and a few logical points up and down the main stem and both branches. No new docks.
- Kinds of the fur-trade river: dugout (pirogue), elm-bark canoe, small plank skiff. Each hull its own length, beam, sheer, rocker and weathering. Textures reuse sheets already loaded in 1812 where possible (phone memory is tight).
- Interact "Cross": step in, row across, step out on the far bank; the boat stays on the far side so it can take you back.
- The fort wharf in photographic weathered timber.
