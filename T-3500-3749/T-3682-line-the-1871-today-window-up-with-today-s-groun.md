## Result (PR #2222)

1871 had no Today registration, and its river (read off Mitchell's 1870 map) runs 20 to 80 m from today's channel. 1871 now registers on the map fit's 36 named corners and on the river's middle every 100 m against the City's waterways polygons (South Branch straightened reach and the mouth left out). Standing on an 1871 bank, Today now opens on today's bank.
