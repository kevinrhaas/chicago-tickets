Bluetooth game controller support: walk, look, jaunts and menus in every year.

Kevin, 2026-11-26 (list item 4, User Experience): *"Controller support: Support Bluetooth gaming controllers."*

**Acceptance:**
- A standard Bluetooth controller (Xbox, PlayStation, Switch Pro or any pad the browser maps to the Standard Gamepad layout) works through the browser's Gamepad API in 1812, 1835, 1871 and 1904, on a computer and on a phone, with no setting to turn on.
- Left stick walks (analogue), right stick looks (scaled by Settings › Look sensitivity), a trigger runs, and while flying the triggers climb and descend.
- A inspects what is under the crosshair; B backs out of what is open; Y flies; X is Interact (gates); LB/RB and D-pad left/right go to the previous and next jaunt stop; Start opens the Navigator; View/Select opens the map.
- Menus: D-pad (or left stick) moves focus between the keys on screen, A presses, B goes back, the right stick scrolls; sliders and pickers change with left and right. The arrival screen and welcome work the same way.
- Using the controller takes over from the mouse or touch the way a touch takes over from the mouse; touching or clicking again hands back.
- The Guide's controls sheet has a Controller sheet listing these buttons.
- Mobile (390×780) and desktop smoke stay at zero page errors.
