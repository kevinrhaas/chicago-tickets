T-2208 (pitched arrises) and T-2209 (shading, roughness) landed, but each stone was still a plateau 15-30 mm in front of its arris, and the granite's material multipliers cooled it to grey.

**Acceptance:**
1. Every street-granite ashlar stone (north, east, west) is pitched to its arris and bowed over a crown, so a raking view along any street wall shows each stone's rounded face and the shadow under it; the light tier carries the bow in its shading without new triangles (it is at its 200,000-triangle cap).
2. The granite reads a light pink-buff like the owner's colour photographs (lit stone red/green above 1, blue/green below 1), not grey.
3. Ships with T-2215 (the entrance) in one Glessner PR: before/after from the published app beside the owner's photographs.
