## Evidence so far (T-2433, 2026-10-13)

Filed by T-2433, which placed Granger Farwell's house at 1623 (the north neighbour of McBirney's 1625 in the 1890 photograph). Farwell's number does not settle Hamline's, and the evidence is split. It is laid out in `chicago/4d/docs/RESEARCH/k01-1623-farwell-2433/README.md` § "What this leaves open at 1619 and 1621":
- **for 1619** (as T-2420 built it): the Inter Ocean, 18 Apr 1886; the 1885 permits' two-lot offset applied to Hamlin's "1621"; and 1621 could be the drawing's driveway, built on later against Farwell's north wall;
- **for 1621**: the 1904 Blue Book's John H. Hamline at 1621; J. Foster Rhodes buying the 1619 lot to build in 1891; the sheet's '4' storeys at 1619 against the drawing's two over a basement, while 1621 is '3B' with a curved bay on the plan.

If it is 1621, T-2420/T-2428 and the open T-2422/T-2429 build Hamline's house at the wrong id, and 1619 becomes the house without a picture (T-2434's premise changes too).
