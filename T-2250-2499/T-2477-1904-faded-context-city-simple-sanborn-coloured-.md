1904 faded context city: simple Sanborn-coloured buildings and trees on every undeveloped part of the map.

Owner, 2026-10-13 (project thread): *"for all the areas we have not developed yet, but are on the map, please include very simple buildings in varied appropriate colors based on Sanborn or other projections, make them just gently faded, still present but it should be obvious that prairie ave and the buildings we are working are in color and those other ones are faded versions of what might be there. I don't want to spend many triangles so simple boxes are ok or very simple single color house structure shapes for the balance of the city, and same with flora for it"*.

Default taken where the ask forks: the 1904 scene first (the year Prairie Avenue lives in); other years can follow. "Faded" = lighter, desaturated, still solid, not transparent.

**Acceptance:**

1. Every lot the 1911 Sanborn sheets 20, 28 and 35 draw off Prairie Avenue (Indiana and Calumet faces, the cross-street lots, Calumet east at 20th) carries its traced building(s) as a faded simple mass: footprint from the sheet, colour family from the sheet's own colour (pink brick, blue stone, yellow frame), height and roof by a stated rule.
2. The rest of the map and a ring beyond it (no sheet in hand) carries a faded projected city: lots on the street pitch the traced grid measures, with the material mix the traced sheets measure, kept off the water and the Illinois Central right-of-way. One numbered liberty covers the projection and the heights.
3. Faded trees on the same lots and along the Indiana and Calumet parkways; nothing on Prairie Avenue's own streets (T-2473..T-2475 own those).
4. A placeholder never stands where a real structure does: one that a placed structure's footprint touches is dropped at load, so drafts that land later (T-2162 and kin) replace them with no regeneration.
5. Cheap: instanced, a handful of draw calls, triangles and phone memory measured before and after at 390x780 and 1280x800; hidden with the confidence view's reconstructed toggle.
