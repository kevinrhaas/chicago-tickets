Every signboard legible and period-lettered: no name hidden by its own wall, period faces, gilt, smalt and carved boards.

**Acceptance:** (state it before working — the definition of done, never weakened to pass)
- No sign's lettering is hidden by its own building: every painted band and fascia lettering stands outside the deepest cladding of the wall it is on, measured by raycasting each flat sign's rectangle against the town's buildings (Newberry & Dole's warehouse, the owner's screenshot, first).
- Every board is lettered in a period face loaded by the page (roman, fat face, Egyptian, grotesque), with the name in a display letter and the trade in a roman or italic, the name on one line and largest; no line overflows its board.
- Gilt boards read as gold leaf (burnished gradient, shade, low roughness); dark painted grounds are smalted (sanded, matte); bare timber boards are carved (incised letters in relief).
- The layer reports every board's lettering size (`signage.legibility`) and the smoke holds it: every board lettered, every line fits, and no flat sign hidden by its wall.
- Phone (390×780) renders every year with no page error and the halved phone atlas still reads.

**Owner report (2026-10-10, Newberry & Dole warehouse on South Water at Franklin):** "This sign I think it cut off at the top, please make sure all signs are period correct and legible, make the letters painted in period fonts and colors correct for the sign and make sure they have excellent texture for the sign if painted or on carved raw wood and lay out the sign name correctly so it fits and is readable and matches period signs of the era, sweep through them all and make them correct".
