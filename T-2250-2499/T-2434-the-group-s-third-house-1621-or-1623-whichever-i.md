## Finding from T-2436 / T-2439 (2026-10-13)

T-2439 rules that John H. Hamline's house — the Building Budget drawing's left-hand house — stood at **1621**, not 1619 (`docs/RESEARCH/hamline-1621-2436/README.md`): the 1885 permit, the 1884-87 directories, the Conzen-Knox 1886 occupant map (1619 Joseph L. McBirney, 1621 John Hamline, 1623 Granger Farwell), the 1886 Robinson plate (its printed '1621' on the first of a matched pair of new houses, sublots 1 and 2) and the 1904 Blue Book all put it there; only the Inter Ocean's 1886 '1619' does not. T-2440 moves T-2420's build to `house_1621_prairie`.
**This ticket's premise changes.** Every house of the attached group 1621-1625 now has a picture (1621 Hamline's drawing, 1623 Farwell's, 1625 McBirney's 1890 photograph). The house without a picture is **1619**: the 1911 sheet's '4' storey stone front with its broad curved bay and a half-round frame piece at the rear, which the 1886 Robinson plate does not draw (it shows a different, stepped-back house on the 33 ft lot, Joseph L. McBirney's) and which is most likely the house J. Foster Rhodes built after paying $18,000 for the lot in July 1891 (Howard E. Perry's in the 1904 Blue Book). After T-2440 hands 1619 back, this ticket is that front: bounded by the sheet, its own and not a copy of a neighbour.
