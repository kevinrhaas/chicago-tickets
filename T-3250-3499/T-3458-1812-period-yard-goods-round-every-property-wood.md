1812: period yard goods round every property, woodpiles, chopping blocks and the working things each house needed.

> Owner, 2026-11-07 (project thread "Clear 1812 worn paths and add yard items"): *"in 1812 you have paths and worn areas around the buildings but there is still grass heavily there, can you minimize or eliminate the grass in the worn paths and areas and add correct photographic and historically correct items around the buildings that likely would be in place, probably wood piles, and other things from the era that are necessary and sensible and correctly placed in a logical area in and around each of the properties"*

**Acceptance:**
- Every 1812 dwelling, the Kinzie working yard, the fort's outer buildings (agency, factory,
  bake house, stables, shops) and Lee's Place carry the yard goods such a place needed in
  August 1812: woodpiles (cordwood ricks, stacked logs), chopping blocks with an axe, a
  sawbuck, water barrels / tubs, a cart or sledge at the working yards, ash hoppers where
  soap was made, kettles over the outdoor fire where cooking was done outside.
- Each placed on the logical side (behind / beside the house, near the door they serve, on
  the worn ground, never in front of the river front or across a path) and refused by the
  structure-clearance gate if it stands in a building or fence.
- Each item is graded: attested only where a source names it at that place; otherwise
  reconstructed with a docs/LIBERTIES.md entry naming what bounds it.
- Photographic: textured wood, bark, iron; reused kit where one exists.
- 1812 phone memory stays under its ceiling (judge against CI's reading).
