Carve the Glessner north-face colonnettes to the front's standard: tapered granite shafts, splayed bases and carved cushion capitals between the two 18th Street window pairs.

Owner, 2026-11-03 (project thread "Fix Glessner north-face window columns", with screenshots of both faces): "looking at the columns around the windows those ones on the north face are different than the ones on the east face that we worked hard to get nice so i think those ones are not right on the north face".

The two 18th Street window pairs (the east-wing gable pair and the tower-shoulder pair) each stand either side of a colonnette. HABS 13 and HABS 14 (1965) show both as the front's kind: a round granite shaft in the wall's own stone, a foot that splays onto the sill, and a carved block capital rounding in to the shaft below; the museum's 2013 post counts nine carved capitals, "front and north", each different. The model draws a pale plain post with a box top and three blades (masonry_house_v4_north_tower.facade), not the T-2965 colonnette.
