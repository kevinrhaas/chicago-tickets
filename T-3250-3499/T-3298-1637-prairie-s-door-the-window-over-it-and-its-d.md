## Owner's report (2026-11-01, project thread "Centre 1637 Prairie entrance bay", with a phone screenshot from the house walk looking east)

> I am fairly sure that the door and windows in the center section here would have been centered on that center section, the are offset to the left. Make sure you handle the front stairs and sidewalk

**Acceptance:** from the house walk, the door, the second-storey window over it and the street dormer over that stand on one axis at the middle of the front between the swell bay's south end (s 5.12) and the wing's north cheek (s 8.3), i.e. s 6.71 rather than 6.25; the stoop and its rails come down on that axis; the iron fence's first post stays at the stoop's south cheek; the flagged house walk leaves the stoop's foot on the same axis and still meets the public walk (walk-joins layer rerun); both match records re-rendered and reviewed.

Checked first against the pictures: the remodel engraving draws the door and the window over it in the middle of that stretch of wall (bay edge x 470 px, window x 502-535 px, wing cheek from x 535-545 px). The 6.25 m reading came through its far camera, which draws the wing's 1.0 m cheek about 21 px wide where the engraving draws it about 75 px. The c.1870 Grosvenor photograph shows only the south-west corner. So centring agrees with the engraving's own proportions.
