Lay the Dearborn drawbridge's lowered draw level.

Owner, 2026-11-03 (project thread "Fix 1835 drawbridge peaked deck"), on a screenshot of the closed draw rising to a ridge at mid-river: *"when the bridge was down did it really have a peak in the center like this, it seems very odd to me that would be the case"*.

T-2375 (PR 1512) gave the closed draw a reconstructed 1.2 m centre rise (`form.draw_profile: peaked`, `form.draw_rise_m: 1.2`) from the one composition that draws it: the Baker-Co engraving (Chicago Tribune, 15 Feb 1880) and Braunhold's redrawing of the same scene in Andreas 1884 p. 631. Both are retrospective, drawn 40+ years after the bridge was chopped up in 1839. No text (Andreas, Moses and Kirkland 1895 "double draw", the 1902 Earle text) gives a rise, and a 1.2 m ridge over a 9.1 m leaf is a 1-in-7.6 grade with a joint at its crest on the town's main team crossing. The lowered draw is laid level with the approaches; the drawings' rise is recorded as an `owner ruling` difference in both match records.
