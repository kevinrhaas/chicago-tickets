1812 horse-drawn wagons in motion: the photographic farm wagon driven by garrison privates on the fort's tracks, loops round the fort and loading runs between gate, factory, wharf and stables on the eve of the evacuation.

## Owner's words (thread "Add horse-drawn wagons to 1812", 2026-11-08)

"I would also like you to add some wagons that are driven by horses and in movement in 1812 just like we have in 1904 but make them the wagons from the era and have some wagons rotating around town on loops, I know in 1812 there are not as many roads but you should I think have people taking wagons places." Then: "We should have some existing photographic wagons that can work for this", "As long as they are appropriate".

## Evidence

- Quaife 1913 (`quaife_1913`) p. 227: the column of 15 August 1812 marched with a wagon train carrying the women and children, defended by the militia, Ronan and Van Voorhis. Wau-Bun (`kinzie_waubun_1856`) 1856 ed. pp. 210/221 names the baggage wagons. So wagons stood at the fort on 14 August, the day before, being loaded.
- Currey 1912 (`currey_1912_story_of_old_fort_dearborn`) p. 87: horses kept in "the stable near the fort"; Helm lists 200 head of cattle.
- No source gives the wagons' make, number, teams (oxen or horses) or drivers. Teams are horses because the town transport has a qualified walking horse and no ox; drivers are garrison privates on fatigue; routes follow the T-3339 worn trails. All reconstructed, one liberty.

## Plan

- Vehicle: the photographic farm-box wagon (Old Cart by Lyskilde, CC BY 4.0; `renderers/web/transport/farm-box-master/`), weathered unpainted timber and iron-tyred spoked wheels, a plain work wagon right for a frontier post. Not the painted W01 or the covered wagons, and not the 1904 brougham.
- Driver: a seated derivative of the 1812 private (`private_first_infantry_1812`) per driver, bound to a named private of Heald's company from the 1812 roll, opening his person card.
- Routes: a new closed-path route kind (polyline with filleted corners) so a wagon follows the worn trails instead of a circle or block, with timed stops (loading) at the gate, factory, wharf and stables.

**Acceptance:**
- In 1812 at Full/Balanced detail, at least two horse-drawn farm wagons move with walking two-horse teams and seated drivers: one looping round the fort, one running between real places with stops.
- Each route keeps to the 1812 worn tracks, clears every building, fence and prop, and passes the existing route preflight (wheel plane, hoof support, clearance).
- Each driver is a named 1812 private, opens his person card, and is not also standing elsewhere.
- Jaunts still show carriages and horses. Light detail and phone memory stay within budget; 390x780 checked.
- Liberty recorded; changelog entry; gate green.
