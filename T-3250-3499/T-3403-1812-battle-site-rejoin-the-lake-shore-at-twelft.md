1812 battle site: rejoin the lake shore at Twelfth Street, raise Heald's high sand bank and its cover, and place his small elevation in the open prairie.

The owner, 2026-11-06 (project thread "Rebuild 1812 battle site shoreline", at stop 9 of the Battle of Fort Dearborn jaunt): *"also in 1812 some land bridge standing out there and the way the descriptions that there was a bank does not really seem to show here from the land, there should be a larger sand bank closer to description ... there is a sharp cut in the lakeshore that does not seem right and you can barely see that whole sand bank, which i think you can make much more significant and accurate in the scene which may need more general flora also to make it accurate and match descriptions from history of the battle site"*; and at stop 10: *"where on the open prairie is the small elevation? you should attempt to cite it and place it based on history and geography and topology adjustments needed"*.

What is wrong now: the 1812 ground reads the foot of Wright's 1834 sheet (its paper edge, which runs back east along N -2112..-2160) as a shore, so a one-row sand causeway runs out into the lake and an islet stands beside it; Rees 1849's shore starts 48-110 m west of Wright's on the same row (post-pier erosion that did not exist in 1812), so the shore steps at a right angle at Twelfth Street; and the "bank" is a 20 m ramp to the 9 ft plain with hummocks of 2-5 ft on it, nothing a visitor can see from the beach. Heald's "small elevation in the open prarie" is not placed at all.

**Acceptance:**
- No land strip or islet off the shore at Twelfth Street; the 1812 shore runs continuously from Wright's pen line to Rees's, with the blend recorded as reconstructed.
- Along Heald's band (1.25-1.75 mi along the march) a beach, then a steep bank face and sand hills 10-20 ft above the water (Moses & Kirkland p. 60), Quaife's row of ridges at about 100 yards, and behind them the "sort of ravine, intervening between the sand-banks and the prairie" (Wau-Bun ch. XIX), each reading quoted and every height and place recorded as a liberty.
- Heald's "small elevation in the open prarie, out of shot of the bank or any other cover" (Heald 1812, Wau-Bun's "rising ground") is placed inland of his mile and a half, with the reasoning for its place recorded; stop 10 of the battle jaunt shows it.
- The bank carries the dune cover the lakeshore zone records (marram and sand reed, juniper "cedars", jack pine, dwarf willows, sand cherry; Wau-Bun's "stunted cedars, pines, and dwarf-willow-trees").
- The 1812 terrain and water GLBs rebaked; stops 9 and 10 checked on desktop and phone (390x780).
