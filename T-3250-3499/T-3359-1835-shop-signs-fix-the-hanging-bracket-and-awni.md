1835 shop signs: fix the hanging bracket and awning boards flat on the facade, beside or over the door.

## The owner's ask (2026-11-03, project thread "Mount 1835 shop signs on facades", on a screenshot of Jones's store)

> you have done a great job on the appearance of the signs in 1835 themselves but i am thinking this whole hanging awning sign thing is not likely, like i have not seen images of that but what is more likely is the sign on the face of the building either put there or painted directly, i think ok to start by sizing and moving most if not all of the hanging signs to ones affixed to logical places either on the front or over the door or on the face like for jones here it could go over the door or on the right or left of the door

This supersedes, for 1835, the 2026-10-10 "chained hanging signs" direction (T-2281).

## Period evidence

- NPS Preservation Brief 25 (Signs): by the end of the 18th century "flat or flush-mounted signs ... had become standard"; fascia signs over the storefront and boards or paint between window levels were the common forms; hanging/projecting signs persisted in the 19th century but "less so than previously", the hanging tradition surviving mainly at public houses.
- This file's own record of the owner's brief: image 5 (Tremont street scene) shows a row of painted fronts; images 6-7 show the Green Tree's board on a post at the corner. No picture in the corpus shows an 1835 Chicago board under an awning hood.

## Acceptance

- No 1835 sign is `bracket_board` or `awning_board`; each former one is a board fixed flat on its own front (`wall_board`), beside the door or over it, clear of every door, window, shutter and frontispiece.
- The six post boards (the Green Tree arrangement, tavern practice) stay.
- Every sign's wording, style, colours and lettering are kept; a flat board may carry the third (place) line its mounting has room for.
- Frame-tavern fronts (frontage scheme) get a Blender-free front-wall reader so a flat board can be placed clear of their openings, held to the builder's literals by a gate.
- Gate green, smoke parts green, desktop and 390x780 phone screenshots before/after.
