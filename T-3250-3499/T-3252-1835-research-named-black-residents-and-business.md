Owner, 2026-10-30 (project thread "Add women of Chicago jaunts"): *"I thought there were black owned business in 1835 you have documented, if so I thought there were several and it would be good to have a tour of that as well, confirm with research and make sure it is documented"*. On the decision card he chose **research first, tour if found**.

What the project holds (docs/RESEARCH/black_chicago_1835.md, T-1377): the corpus counts the free Black town twice (Caton's six or seven certificates of freedom, August 1833; 53 free coloured persons in 1840) and names it never before 1840. The two Black-owned firms (rcb_fb_barbers_shop, rcb_fb_washing_and_ironing) are reconstructed and unplaceable. A 2026-10-30 web check found the same: the community took root in the 1840s (John Jones, 1845).

**Do:** read Christopher Robert Reed, "The Early African American Settlement of Chicago, 1833-1870", JISHS 108 (2015), and Black Chicago's First Century (2005), plus any reachable Cook County Commissioners' Court record of the 1833 certificates. Record every named Black resident or business before 1 July 1835 with its source, or record the negative search.

**Acceptance:** findings written to docs/RESEARCH/black_chicago_1835.md with source records; a jaunt is built only if at least four businesses or homes can be placed in the scene from sources; otherwise the owner is told plainly what was found.
