1835: every dooryard garden its own size, beds and fence, sized to the house and household.

The owner, 2026-11-07, after the 1812 garden seasons (T-3460): *"now that you have a knowledge of how to plant gardens, can you do a sweep of 1812 and 1835 and improve all of the gardens? one of the things in 1835 is that all of the gardens seem to look identical with identical fencing so all of those should vary, all of the gardens should be unique, not necessarily wildly different crops but different sizes and layouts that are sensible for the design of the property and the family and team at hand, like I think Lees place had a large garden? and kinize? and so many places in 1835."*

**Acceptance:**
- No two 1835 dooryard gardens share a size, bed layout and fence: area, depth, bed count and arrangement follow the lot, the house's size and the household (a larger house or a bigger family gets more ground), all seeded per lot so they hold still between loads.
- The garden fences vary by property (picket, paled, board, rail; post spacing, height, gate place), each drawn from forms the town already attests or that its liberty states.
- The 1835 gardens are planted with crops (the gardens layer, as 1812's), in an early-July stage that suits the scene's date and follows Settings > Season.
- Phone memory and triangle ceilings hold; the gate green; desktop and phone screenshots; every invented part a recorded liberty.
