1812 jaunt: the Battle of Fort Dearborn, April to August 1812, in 12 stops.

The owner, 2026-11-03 (project thread "Add 1812 Fort Dearborn story jaunts"): *"Can you render a battle of fort Dearborn jaunt showing all the key sites and a date journey, lees hardscrabble, etc the fort, where kinzie family held out, the site of the conflict, for example, include all of the historical location story points maybe up to 10-12 stops. You can fly or go instantly for places that are far away ... give this good storytelling and narrative these are all rich and compelling stories that must be expressed in the jaunts, do your research and include quotes and rich details from sources"*; and *"This is for 1812"*.

**Acceptance:**
- A 1812 jaunt in `data/jaunts/`, 10-12 stops in date order from the killings at Lee's Place (6 April 1812) through the order to evacuate, the councils, the destroyed arms and liquor, the march of 15 August, the attack on the lake shore, the Kinzie house and Ouilmette's on the 16th, to the burning of the fort.
- Each stop dated, told from the sources with verbatim quotes in attested claims (Heald's report, Wau-Bun, Quaife 1913, Moses and Kirkland, Kirkland 1893, Currey 1912), and the places where the accounts disagree said so.
- Far stops by Fly or Instantly; stops are scene anchors placed clear of trees with a view of their subject (checked in the browser).
- Compiles and passes the jaunt refusals (no Indigenous encounter staged, quotes verbatim); phone 390x780 and desktop checked.
