1812 battle site: carry the battle shore south to Eighteenth Street and move battle jaunt stops 9 and 10 to the 17th-20th Street ground.

The owner, 2026-11-07 (project thread "Validate Fort Dearborn battle site location"): *"you position stop 9 and 10 of the battle of fort dearborn roughly 1100 south along the lake, and from what i understood it was further south around 18th ... if we need to move the site due to proven sources or research thats ok and just handle all of the shore and elevation work we did, i want the sites to be accurate"*. After the research note (https://claude.ai/artifact/Uug2tDykaDToh4xqj1nAbN) he chose "Move to 17th-20th St", and added: *"keep that beachy sandy shore and elevations and ridges along there that you already built its nice so dont destroy it, that would be the accurate shoreline it seems"* and *"and the trees, etc"*.

What is wrong now: stops 9 and 10 stand at about 10th-11th Street, where T-3403 measured Heald's "about a mile and a half" along the wiggling traced waterline. Heald's own journal says "two miles from the Fort" (Quaife 1913 App. III), and every account that names a place lands at 16th-21st Street: the Kinzie family's cottonwoods in 18th Street and Arnold's "moved south and west" (Andreas 1884 p. 31), an Indian's 1840 statement (ibid.), Fernando Jones's 1835 pointers, the 17th & Prairie burials and the 1836 grave with U.S. buttons at 21st & Indiana (Kirkland 1893), Harpell's relics (Andreas p. 31). Musham 1943's Roosevelt Road is the minority reading.

**Acceptance:**
- `battle_bank_1812` carried south to about 20th Street; the beach, bank, ridges, ravine and dune trees T-3403 built north of 16th Street are unchanged (owner's ruling).
- Heald's small elevation placed inland and south-west of the bank near 19th-20th Street and Indiana Avenue; the T-3403 rise near 10th Street kept as ground (owner's ruling), no longer labelled Heald's.
- Stop 9 stands on the beach at about 17th Street looking toward the bank and the Eighteenth Street cottonwoods; stop 10 below the new rise; both checked on desktop and phone (390x780), no tree in the middle of the view.
- Stops 9 and 10, their claims, legs, hours (Helm's "about 10 of the Clock") and since-leads rewritten; the scene card "Where the attack happened" weighs the readings and names Roosevelt Road as the minority reading.
- data/terrain/1812_evacuation_readings.json gains Heald's journal, Helm, Woodward, the Western Courier, Fernando Jones, Harpell and Arnold, verbatim with locators; the derived route re-derived.
- Liberty L-t3403-battle-shore-1812 updated; the 1812 terrain rebaked.
