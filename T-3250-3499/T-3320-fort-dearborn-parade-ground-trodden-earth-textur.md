Fort Dearborn parade ground: trodden-earth texture that feathers into the grass, 1812 and 1835.

The owner, 2026-11-02 (project thread "Add interactable fort gates", with a screenshot of the 1835 parade): *"can you improve the texture of the ground so it is not a solid color but a more realistic texture that looks good and also blur/gradient all the transitions from that to grass or dirt so there are not sharp lines dividing ground surfaces"*

Found before work: both forts' parades are the fort_structure archetype's 6 cm proud box in one flat wall colour, so they read as a solid beige slab with a hard edge against the sward and the building strips. The fort's apron outside the walls already draws as the yards layer's `trodden_earth` (enclosure record fort_dearborn_apron, generated by tools/generate_fort_apron.py) with a feathered fringe, and looks right; 1812 draws no enclosures layer at all.

**Acceptance:**
- 1835 and 1812: the parade reads as textured trodden earth (the yards layer's own treatment and tiles), not a flat colour, and its edge gives way to the grass over a soft, irregular feather with no hard line; the slab no longer draws.
- The parade ground is a derived record (generated from the parade sidecars, re-derived byte for byte by check.sh), graded reconstructed, with a liberty stating the claim and its bounds.
- The sun-dial and flagstaff still stand; the walker still walks the parade; the apron is unchanged.
- Phone (390x780) and desktop checked in both years; gate check green.
