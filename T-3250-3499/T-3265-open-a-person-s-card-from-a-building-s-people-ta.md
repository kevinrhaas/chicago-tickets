Open a person's card from a building's People tab, with Back to the household.

The owner, 2026-10-30, on Glessner's People tab (1904): *"if you click on people a person it goes to a full list and there is no way to get back, like on august billings or anyone. it should go to a detailed card on that person and you should be able to go back to the household"*, and *"the card should have their detail and explain who they are, tell their interesting story and explain the sourcing also, like why some are attested and why some are inferred, but focus mostly on the person and their story"*.

**Acceptance:**
1. Tapping anyone on a building's People tab (every 1904 building that lists people) opens that person's own card in place, inside Inspect; it never jumps to the full People list.
2. The card leads with the person: name, place in the household, work, born / age in 1904 / birthplace / came to America where the record states them, and their story in the record's own words.
3. A "How we know" part says why they are attested or inferred (the 1900 census four years before the scene; the 1904 Blue Book; the museum's pages), shows the census line as written with a link to the page image where the record names the leaf, and lists the sources by title.
4. A Back control on the card returns to the household list, on the person just read.
5. The People app's rows and map pins open the same card (on the person's house) rather than a bare list.
6. Phone (390x780) and desktop screenshots; gate and smoke green.
