Winter street elms draw as bare round branches, not brown twig-card fans.

Owner (Kevin, 2026-11-06), on dev, 1904 Prairie / E 18th St with Season set to Winter:
"the winter trees are not bare branches like we expect, but are odd brown fans", then
"maybe they would look better if the branch bases connected and there was actual texture
to them and they were not flat".

Cause: T-1934 (PRs 1564, 1566, 1578) drew a bare parkway elm past the 10 m near ring as
painted twig-spray cards (leaf atlas row 6) plus three-sided spikes from each skipped
branch's foot to its tip. The cards read as flat brown fans and the spikes as flat wedges
standing off the limbs. On the phone's tier every elm is that mid template.

**Acceptance:**
- No bare elm (parkway vase elm and the young/limbed/high forms) draws a twig card at any ring.
- At the mid ring (10-44 m, and every elm on the phone's tier) the winter wood is round,
  bark-mapped tube: every kept limb and branch, then the fringe twig fork by fork, each twig
  rooted on a drawn parent so none floats.
- At the far ring the branches are seated on the limbs as drawn (no floating strokes).
- Summer, spring and fall templates unchanged; winter cost stated (triangles per template,
  spawn delta) and phone memory not pushed over its budgets.
- Before/after at desktop and phone width.
