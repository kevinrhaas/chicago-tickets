1812: plant the commanding officer's garden south of the fort (Whistler's beds and walks, mid-August crops) and hang a garden gate a visitor can open and walk through.

The owner, 2026-11-06 (project thread "Plant the 1812 commanding officer's garden", on stop 9 of the Fort Dearborn Building by Building jaunt): *"and you describe this awesome garden for 1812 but nothing is in the garden"*, and *"and also cant seem to enter the garden"* (his screenshot: the gate is a grey untextured box frame with a black void where the opening should be).

Sources in hand: Whistler's 1808 draught (Quaife 1913 facing p. 164) draws the garden as ten beds with clipped corners: a border bed either side of the entry walk along the north paling, then eight beds in two rows of four, a walk round them, a wider cross walk each way, and quarter-round corner beds at the south corners. Quaife 1913 (ch. on the first fort): *"melons and other small fruit and vegetables were raised"*; and Private Sendale *"worked hard all day in the Colonel's garden"* (23 May 1811). M'Mahon's American Gardener's Calendar (August, kitchen garden): melons and cucumbers fruiting, cabbages and savoys, beans, celery earthed up, turnips and late peas sown, herbs cut.

**Acceptance:**
- The garden's beds and walks are laid as the draught draws them, at its lettered 50 ft scale, inside the existing paling; the beds are tilled earth and the walks trodden.
- The beds are planted for 14 August 1812, varied and photographic, not textures on the ground: melons (attested) with fruit on the vine, small fruit (currant and gooseberry bushes, raspberry canes, strawberries) along the north border, and vegetables (cabbages, pole beans, Indian corn, squash, potatoes, beets, carrots, onions with tops falling, herbs). Every crop beyond melons and small fruit is reconstructed and claimed as a liberty.
- The gate is a paling wicket on two posts, not the fort's heavy frame; it opens and shuts with Interact (R) and a visitor can walk in and around the walks; the paling is a wall again.
- Phone (390x780) boots 1812 within its memory budget; desktop and phone screenshots of stop 9 and inside the garden, before and after.
