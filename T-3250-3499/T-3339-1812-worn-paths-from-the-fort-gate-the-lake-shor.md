1812: worn paths from the fort gate, the lake-shore trail and the Vincennes trace, and worn yards round the buildings.

The owner, 2026-11-03 (project thread "Add 1812 worn paths and trees"): *"in 1812 I get that there are not formal roads but I would expect worn paths almost roads from the fort entrance and even out south, I thought there was the road to Vincennes, so can you add natural worn paths and worn areas around the buildings that would be sensible and paths connecting, vary them based on density and some can be very light."* And: *"for streets and roads for 1812 do whatever research you can to make them accurate and don't overdo it but be sensible and logical and make it look natural."*

Sources read for it: Andreas 1884 v1 ("Along the lake shore ran a beaten Indian trail, which was the path pursued", the evacuation; the Hardscrabble cabin "near the most frequented trail that led to the Desplaines"); Currey 1912 (the march "along the shore of the lake ... In the absence of roads"; Lee's Place escape run along the South Branch and the main stem's south bank to the fort, calling across to Burns near the State Street bridge; Mrs. Kinzie's run from Burns's to her own house); Quaife 1923, *Chicago's Highways Old and New* ch. III (the Vincennes Trace an ancient Potawatomi trail; Heald rode it in 1811; the Wabash bands came up it in August 1812; Hubbard's trail and the 1834 State road followed it).

**Acceptance:**
- 1812 draws worn earth paths, varying in wear by traffic: a well-worn way from the fort's south gate, the lake-shore trail south past the Lee house and the outlet, the Vincennes trace south on the sand plain (the 1835 State road's reconstructed line), the south-bank path west to the forks and on along the South Branch towards Lee's Place, the north-bank path Kinzie house to Burns, and short faint paths to the outer buildings, gardens and landings.
- Worn yards (trodden/worn earth feathered into the grass) round the outer fort buildings, the Kinzie compound and the settlers' houses; faint ones where traffic was light.
- Everything generated from data by a gated command (re-derived in check.sh); alignments reconstructed and recorded as a liberty; existence cited.
- No path or yard on water or inside a structure; nothing touches the parade ground (T-3320).
- 1812 phone and desktop memory no worse than dev by more than a few MB; desktop and phone screenshots before/after.
