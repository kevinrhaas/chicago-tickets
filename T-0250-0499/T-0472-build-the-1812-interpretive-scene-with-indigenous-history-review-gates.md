Create the 1812 scene/date and evidence cards around the event commonly called the Battle of Fort Dearborn or Fort Dearborn Massacre. The scene may ship terrain, structures, route geography, documentary text and non-human environmental evidence before any human depiction is authorized.

AGENTS.md's standing Indigenous-history constraint applies with unusual force here: do not improvise Native presence, dialogue, clothing, tactics or individual depictions. Begin with the Newberry's Indigenous Chicago materials and seek Native-scholar/community review before any such representation. Present contested terminology and competing location claims explicitly rather than selecting a triumphalist narrative.

Research starting points: Newberry Indigenous Chicago curriculum; Illinois State Historical Society Fort Dearborn marker material; Chicago History Museum holdings; primary accounts already present in the Fort Dearborn research corpus.

Acceptance: `data/scenes/1812.json` (or the project's chosen dated id) resolves to the first fort and 1812 terrain; cards explain evidence/conflicts and terminology; `review_required` is applied where required; the scene remains valid and useful with no human figures; and no 1835-only structures appear.
