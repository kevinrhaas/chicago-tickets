## The evidence

- Surgeon Cooper, at the post from 1808, counted four houses on the north side, "occupied by Kinzie, Ouilmette, La Lime, and Le Mai" (Quaife 1913 p. 166); La Lime "was living in one of the four houses on the north side of the river and acting as government Indian interpreter" (pp. 148-50). He and Dr. Smith wintered 1803-04 in a vacant cabin there (p. 142).
- Whistler's draught (Jan. 1808) draws three houses west of Kinzie's, each with a '31', "Oather Dwelling Houses on opisite side River", unnamed (`data/traces/whistler_1808_fort_dearborn.json` index[30]). Quaife pairs them with Cooper's names.
- Le Mai had sold the du Sable house to Kinzie in 1803-04, so Cooper's Le Mai of 1808 is in another house.
- La Lime was killed in the spring of 1812; nothing read says what became of his house. Standing on the scene date is inferred from continuity: say so on the record.
- Mrs. Callis puts a house "occupied in 1816 by a Mr. Bridges" between Kinzie's and the Burns house (Andreas p. 89). Weigh whether it is one of the '31' houses; it is not dated before 1816 and is not built on that alone.

## What to build

The two '31' houses that T-2486 does not take for Ouilmette's: seat all three with T-2062's outside-the-stockade rule (the same frame and expansion as the agent's house), decide WITH T-2486 which is Ouilmette's ("close in the rear of the Kinzie house"), and give the other two to La Lime and Le Mai with the assignment marked `reconstructed` (Cooper names the occupants, the sheet names no house). Log dwellings at the draught's drawn size; anything the sheet does not give is a liberty. Cards: La Lime's names him as the post's interpreter and his death in spring 1812 without depicting it; Le Mai's household was Métis (Quaife: "a half-breed, married to a Pottawatomie" woman) and carries `review_required` + `touches_removal` with its sentence.

Waits for T-2486 (same ground, one PR at a time).

**Acceptance:** two log houses stand west of Kinzie's in the 1812 scene, at the draught's '31' places, each card citing Quaife's Cooper and the draught, with the occupant assignment graded `reconstructed` and its liberty numbered; desktop and 390x780 shots, no page errors.
